SCAN TRAINER

Lock the waveforms to scan the contact

Scan a contact

Time0.0s
Best—

After the lock

  • A primary scan gives the contact’s faction, class, hull, shields and systems. Shields read as numbers separated by colons, one per projector, numbered clockwise from the front: call them as “Shield 2 down to 71”.
  • A secondary scan goes deeper: the exact signals, internal systems, beam firing arcs, and the shield and beam frequencies.
  • More scanner power makes the lock easier and quicker. Ask Engineering for a scanner spike when the scan matters, not because the button exists.
