How it works

  • Every scan has two stages. In each, move the slider until the noisy red, green and blue signal pulls together into one clean wave, then hold it there until it locks.
  • Level 1 has one slider per stage; level 2 has two, and the signal only comes clean when both are right.
  • A primary scan gives the contact’s faction, class, hull, shields and systems; a secondary scan goes deeper, to the exact signals, internal systems, beam firing arcs, and the shield and beam frequencies.
  • More scanner power makes the lock easier and quicker. Ask Engineering for a scanner spike when the scan matters.
