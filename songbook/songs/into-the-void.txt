Lay down
Your sweet and weary head
Night has fallen
You have come to journey's end
Sleep now
As you meet the ones who came before
They are calling
From beyond the stars

Please do not weep
Have not these tears upon your face?
For you were brave
For you braver than you know
Sibling in arms
You can sleep long now

Chorus:
What can you see
Out in those stars?
What was your call to explore?
Across the void
The nebula gleam
The ships have come to carry you home

And all will turn
Back to stardust
A light in the darkness
All souls pass

Set sail
Into the world of night
Through darkness falling
Out of scanner range and sight

Don't say
We have come now to the end
The unknown's calling
You and I will meet again
Beloved sibling in arms
Just sleep now

Repeat chorus

And all will turn
Back to stardust
A light in the darkness
Blue ships pass

Into the void
