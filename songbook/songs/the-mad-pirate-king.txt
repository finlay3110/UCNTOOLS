He's sweaty
He's smelly
He shows off his belly
When challenged
He puts up his dukes
For he loves a good brawl
When his back's 'gainst the wall
Just be careful if you give him nukes!

If you're in for a pound
He'll be in for his Penny
And he won't give up ground
Like the all times when he
Defended 5-5
Kept Kalgoorlie alive!
And he blew up those vipers
In mass Xenocide!

Chuck Richards don't know what he's thinking
Especially when he's been out drinking!
With a roll of the die
Might give Bell a black eye,
Or become a great asset to UCNI

Now, he may not be sane
And our man fears no pain
But love him or loathe him, you all know his name

So raise up your glasses and join as I sing!
For Gobba! The mad pirate king!

He once was despised
Now we toast him with pride
And we thank the gods, Gobba is now on our side!
