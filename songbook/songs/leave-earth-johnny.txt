I thought I heard the Admiral say:
"Leave Earth, Johnny, leave Earth."
Tomorrow we will find our way,
and it's time for us to leave Earth.

Chorus:
Leave Earth, Johnny, leave Earth!
Oh, leave Earth, Johnny, leave Earth!
For the skies are dark and the grass won't grow
And it's time for us to leave Earth.

Oh, the warnings came but we stayed too long.
Leave Earth, Johnny, leave Earth!
The scientists said it all along.
And it's time for us to leave Earth.

Repeat chorus

Look at the size of the Exodus fleet!
Leave Earth, Johnny, leave Earth!
Five factions sailing in retreat
And it's time for us to leave Earth.

Repeat chorus

We sail into the void to start anew.
Leave Earth, Johnny, leave Earth!
The Adamas Belt is what we pursue.
And it's time for us to leave Earth.

Repeat chorus
