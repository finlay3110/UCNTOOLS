I've been a young ensign for many a run
And I've propped up the bar drinking whisky and rum
But now I'm promoted, new stripes to be worn
And I never will be a young ensign no more

Chorus:
And it's no, nay, never
No never no more
Will I be a young ensign
No never, no more

With fresh new adventures those stripes they grew wide
From sparring with pirates and others beside
The pay is no better, despite what you'd think
At least from the captain you get a free drink

Repeat chorus

In time I'm approved by the Admiralty board
Kind words from the captain the greatest reward
But lieutenant commanders we often do fret
For we have to look after the Oldest Cadet!

Repeat chorus
