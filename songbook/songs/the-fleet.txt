title: The Fleet
category: Patriotic Songs
words: Lt. Cmdr. Jim - Ship's Councillor
tune: Marching Through Georgia; Henry Clay Work

Sound the call to battle stations, sound it far and wide
Flanks speed forth to combat now, our crewmates by our side
Our enemies will flee in vain, there's nowhere they can hide
From the fleet that fights for all peoples

Chorus:
The fleet, the fleet
It's Roger Taylor's fleet
The fleet, the fleet
A force that can't be beat
We've orders to defend the belt, no chance of a retreat
By the fleet that fights for all peoples

Hark! The signals coming from the east and from the west
Our allies now are warping in, we fight with renewed zest
United we shall stand together, formed up from the best
As a fleet that is drawn from all peoples

Repeat chorus

We stand foursquare for liberty, all peoples must be free
None of us shall falter from the fight, tho' stern it be
And the Cobalt Flag we love shall fly for all to see
'Cross a belt that is free for all peoples

Repeat chorus

The fleet, it now is charging on, the battle to begin,
Our voices now are raised on high above the battle's din,
We'll never cease from fighting 'til the victory we win,
And the belt is free for all peoples

Repeat chorus
