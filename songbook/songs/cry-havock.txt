id: 11
title: Cry Havock
category: Bar/Drinking Songs
words: Dr. O. Nightingale MD
tune: Any fitting the Common Metre
source_book: UCN Songbook
source_page: 12
---
The Havock is a noble ship,
A ship of high renown,
The darling of the UCN,
The jewel in the crown,
The pride of every engineer,
The captain's favourite craft,
Destroyers try to pass her
And all we do is laugh!

Chorus:
(And it's)
Strike swift, strike sure,
Wheel about, come in again and
Strike hard, strike true
And send them on their way.

Pirates come for plunder,
They think they've won their prize
But the Havock's right behind them
What a terrible surprise!
Springing from the nebula,
With thunder in her eye,
You can see the starlight glinting,
You can hear the gunners' cry!

Repeat chorus

And when we're on Arcadia
The bars kick up a fuss
You've never seen what Havock means
Until you've drunk with us,
But when they say 'why won't you stay?'
We all reply 'What for?
We Havocks all are barking mad
Cos we're the dogs of war!'

Repeat chorus
