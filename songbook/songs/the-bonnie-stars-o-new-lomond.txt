id: 32
title: The Bonnie Stars o' New Lomond
category: Bar/Drinking Songs
words: Sub Lt. Tetra
tune: The Bonnie Banks o' Loch Lomond; trad
source_book: UCN Songbook
source_page: 35
---
By yon starlit docks and yon bright orbital rings,
Where the cold suns shine o'er New Lomond,
Where me and my true crew were ever wont tae roam,
On the bonnie, bonnie stars o' New Lomond.

Chorus:
Oh ye'll take the warp lane, and I'll take the impulse lane,
And I'll be in Sol afore ye,
But me and my true crew may never meet again,
On the bonnie, bonnie stars o' New Lomond.

'Twas there that we parted in yon silent void,
Beside Havock's shuttle gliding,
Where the nebulae burned in soft violet hue,
And the pale moons watched us hiding.

Repeat chorus

The comm beacons fade and the great warp core hums,
And the starfields lie cold and sleeping,
But the weary heart kens nae second dawn again,
Though the lost may cease from their grieving.

Repeat chorus

For the void calls on through the endless black seas,
And the fleet still sails through the silence,
With the old flags worn and the hull plating scarred,
Still we answer the stars with defiance.

Repeat chorus

Oh ye'll take the warp lane, and I'll take the impulse lane,
And I'll be in Sol afore ye,
But me and my true crew may never meet again,
On the bonnie, bonnie stars o' New Lomond.
