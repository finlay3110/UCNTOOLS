A good ship full of trusty hands
Well train-ed, brave and true
The President shall understand
What our cadets can do
No matter when or where we go
Across the inky black
Through peril bleak and sneak attack
We'll bring our [Ship Name] back

Chorus:
And shall the [Ship Name] live
Or shall the [Ship Name] die
Officers of the UCN
Shall know the reason why

A pirate Captain brave and bold
Our shuttle eye'd with glee
In tractor beam that bird was hold
We'll set our shipmates free

We'll cross the belt, grid to grid
Adamas is no stay
We'll "go beyond" in unit-ay
Our enemies to slay

Repeat chorus

And when we come to their foul lair
All under great attack
No quarter will our captain give
Until our shipmate's back

Now [Ship Name] is a tough old bird
But the damage is quite great
Blown OCPs and safety smoke
Signal a dire fate

Repeat chorus

[Alternative blame chorus:]
And shall the [Ship Name] live
Or shall the [Ship Name] die
These green cadets - dead OCPs
Shall be the reason why
