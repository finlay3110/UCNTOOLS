Here's forty credits on the drum,
For those who'll volunteer to come,
To 'list and fight pirates today,
Over the belt and far away.

Chorus:
O'er teleport and through Warspite
With our crews all ready; all trained right
The fleet commands and we obey
Out through the belt and far away

It's gravium we seek of course
It is a limited resource
Its import we cannot gainsay
Out in the belt and far away

Repeat chorus

When FO calls me, I must go,
To fly and face another foe,
Adamas have seen our ships of war,
Now friends and trading shall endure

Repeat chorus

To Red alert, we fight as one
Our beams a-blazing like a sun
Warping out to come what may
Out in the belt and far away

Repeat chorus

When JUDITH calls me, I must go
The warp core burned out long ago
'til we're picked up I guess we'll stay
Out in the belt and far away

Repeat chorus

If destroyed and to return no more
As many comrades did before,
In the mess celebrations should play,
Out through the belt and far away.

Repeat chorus

To go beyond is our refrain
Earth, Luna, Mars and more again
The fleet commands and we obey
Out through the belt and far away

Repeat chorus

Our target relative will be
To Gliese six sixty seven C

Out to the belt and far away

For those that volunteer to come

Over the belt and far away

Craft Corner:
When not deployed some like to chill
With mastery of crafting skill
To knit, to paint, perhaps crochet?
Over the belt and far away

The Teleporter:
The teleporter's safe, it's true
But now my hair's a fetching blue
A minor glitch that we'll downplay
Over the belt and far away
