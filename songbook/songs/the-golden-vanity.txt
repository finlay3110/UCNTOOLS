title: The Golden Vanity

There was a bonny ship from the northern country
The name of the ship was the Golden Vanity
One day they were hunted by the Spanish enemy
To sink her in the lowland, lowland, low

Chorus:
Sink her in the lowland sea
Lowland, lowland, sink her in the lowland sea

Well up spoke the cabin boy, a gallant heart had he
Tell me captain what is it that you will give to me
If I were to swim up to the Spanish enemy
And sink her in the lowland, lowland, low

Repeat chorus

Well if you were to sink her, if you were to be so bold,
I will give you riches and my daughter's hand to hold,
You will live in wedded bliss until you both grow old
So sink her in the lowland, lowland, low

Repeat chorus

Then the boy made ready and overboard sprang he
And he swam with all his might to the Spanish enemy
Drilled six holes into her hull and then another three
To sink her in the lowland, lowland, low

Repeat chorus

Then quickly he swam back to the cheering of the crew
But the captain would not heed him, his promise he did rue
And he scorned his poor entreatings when loudly he did sue
And left him in the lowland, lowland, low

Repeat chorus

Then his messmates drew him up, but on the deck he died
They wrapped him in his hammock, it was so long and wide,
They cast the gallant cabin boy over the ship's side,
And he sank down in the Lowland, Lowland, low

Repeat chorus
