Now Lukey's boat was painted green,
A-ha, me boys!
Lukey's new boat was painted green,
The prettiest boat that you've ever seen,
A-ha, me boys riddle-i-day!

Now the Vicar christened her boat Bunny,
A-ha, me boys!
The Vicar christened her boat Bunny,
For rabbits and vicars they just ain't funny
A-ha, me boys riddle-i-day!

It was Friday morn when he put to sea
A-ha, me boys!
A Friday morn when he went to sea
He took a pasty for his tea
A-ha, me boys riddle-i-day!

Now Lukey steamed down in the bay
A-ha, me boys!
Lukey steamed down in the bay
To catch some lobsters through the day
A-ha, me boys riddle-i-day!

Now Lukey had a fine great catch,
A-ha, me boys!
Lukey had a fine great catch,
The lobsters filled up through the hatch,
A-ha, me boys riddle-i-day!

Now Lukey didn't know it were a sin
A-ha, me boys!
Lukey didn't know it were a sin
To whistle at sea cos you're whistling the wind
A-ha, me boys riddle-i-day!

Now a tonne of lobsters filled the boat
A-ha, me boys!
A tonne of lobsters filled the boat
The poor old bunny just couldn't float
A-ha, me boys riddle-i-day!

The bunny sank into the blue
A-ha, me boys!
The bunny sank into the blue
The pasty, the lobsters and the vicar too
A-ha, me boys riddle-i-day!

Now Lukey's new boat was painted green,
A-ha, me boys!
Lukey's new boat was painted green,
The unluckiest boat that you've ever seen,
A-ha, me boys riddle-i-day!
