[v1 - Arrival at Gliese 667Cc]

Join me my friends, join with me.
As we fly ever on, from that we know
Away from our homes, on land or in space
Let us sing for those gone far behind us

For all of that beauty and joy we left
Wild blue oceans, and stars above
For all those we knew, and those that won't
We sing of our hope that something must go on

Join me my friends, join with me
As we fly ever on, from that we know
Away from our homes, to something new
Let our song give us hope for we must press on,

Join me my friends, join with me, in song

[v2 - Returning to the Adamas]

Join me my friends, join with me.
As we fly ever on, for new discoveries
Away from our homes, on land or in space
Let us sing of that system we hope to aid

Far out into the void of space,
Lies the trinary that we once knew
For all it may aid us, it is still far
And the journey is long but we do it for home

Join me my friends, join with me
As we fly ever on, to new mysteries,
Back to the Adamas, back to our hope,
Let us sing of new sights and treasures beyond

Join me my friends, join with me, back there

[v3 - Back in the Adamas]

Join me my friends, join with me.
As we fly ever on, for new discoveries
Finding a new home, out among friends
Let us sing of that system we hope to aid

From wild blue oceans, red sands or stars
We all share a bond that can't be torn apart
Sing for our fellows both new and old
For space takes us all no matter how bold

Join me my friends, join with me
As we fly ever on, to new mysteries,
Back in the Adamas, back to our hope,
Let us sing with our new friends and for discoveries

Join me my friends, join with me, out there
