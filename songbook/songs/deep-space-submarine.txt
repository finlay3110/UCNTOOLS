When we're damaged in a fight
it upsets the engineer
but the risk we might implode
means they'll do what they most fear

Once the switch has been engaged
we'd better hope we don't have to flee
'coz without a quantum drive
our top speed is strictly sub-c

But at least we should be glad
that the designers installed some beds
we would have a crummy time
