id: 89
title: Those Who’ve Gone Beyond
category: Bar/Drinking Songs
words: Dr. O. Nightingale MD
tune: One More Pull; Ian Woods
---
Chorus
Now before our watch is over
And before our final song, 
Pass the can round, fill your glasses, 
For those who've gone beyond.

Sign the watch log, turn it over,
Hold the course and strike the bell, 
Pass the roll, and take the muster
Of the names we knew so well. 

Repeat Chorus

Pull your boots off, turn your bunks down, 
But before we go to sleep
We'll remember all our messmates
Who no more their watch will keep.

Repeat Chorus

And no matter where we roam, mates,
And no matter near or far, 
We are never far from home, mates;
We have friends among the stars. 

Repeat Chorus
