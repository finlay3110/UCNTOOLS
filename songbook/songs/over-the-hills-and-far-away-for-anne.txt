title: Over the Hills and Far Away (for Anne)
category: Traditional Songs
words: Traditional
tune: Over the Hills and Far Away; trad
source_book: Traditional Songs

---
Hark! Now the drums beat up again
We're all true soldiers gentlemen
Then let us 'list and march I say
Over the hills and far away

Over the hills and o'er the main
To Flanders, Portugal and Spain
Queen Anne commands and we'll obey
Over the hills and far away

Gentlemen who have a mind
To serve a Queen both good and kind
To 'list and enter into pay
Then over the hills and far away

No more sound from drum retreat
With Marlborough and Gallaway beat
French and Spaniards every day
When o'er the hills and far away

Forty shillings on the drum
For those who volunteer to come
With shirts and clothes and pleasant pay
When over the hills and far away

The' 'Prentice Tom he may refuse
To wipe his angry master's shoes
For then he's free to sing and play
Over the hills and far away

We then shall lead more happy lives
By getting rid of brats and wives
That scold on both the night and day
Over the hills and far away

For if we go 'tis one to ten
But we return all gentlemen
Gentlemen as well as they
But over the hills and far away
