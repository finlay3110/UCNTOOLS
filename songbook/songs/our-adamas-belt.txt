Do you see
The navy warping in?
With the cocksure confidence
That they will always win
As if they never struggled
Never had to go without
Our forefathers were abandoned here
Eking out, year on year
Theirs returned to plenty,
while ours survived the drought.

Oh
Do you see,
the UCN?
They were gone and now the bluebacks are back again
Making deals and taking space
with the stroke of a pen
While we made do with the hand we were dealt
But they'll never understand it
Never quite command it
Because it's our Adamas Belt

Our Adamas Belt
Its beauty and its dangers
Don't belong to strangers
For forty years, we alone have dwelt
So, UCN, don't you see?
You can't take it all from me
Because it's my Adamas, our Adamas Belt
