Tongues of fire from stellar flaring
News of foemen near declaring
To heroic deeds of daring
Call you, UCN

Groans of wounded people dying
Wails of parents and kids flying
For the distant succour crying
Call you, UCN

Shall the voice of wailing
Now be unavailing
You to rouse who never yet
In battle's hour were failing

This our answer, ships in warping
Swift as stellar winds a-roaring
Not in vain the voice imploring
Calls the UCN

Loud the red alert is sounding
Hearts of every crew are bounding
Captains briefing plans astounding
Fly we, UCN

Short, the sleep the foe is taking
Ere the morrow's morn is breaking
They shall have a rude awakening
From the UCN
