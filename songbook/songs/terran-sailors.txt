Farewell and adieu to you fair Terran beauties
Farewell and adieu to you beauties of Earth
For we've received orders to deploy to Adamas
But we hope very soon we shall see you again

Chorus:
We'll rant and we'll roar, like true Terran sailors
We'll rant and we'll roar, wherever we'll be
Some day we'll return to Sol's warm gentle sunlight
But for now we set course for six six seven C

We hove our ship to, to check diagnostics
Our engines complaining, and threat'ning to break
Deploying our SPANRs, and judicious percussions
Until Captain ordered us - best speed to make

Repeat chorus

Our course we did set, to skirt round the barrens
Then Xanadu station, alone in the night
We sailed on by miners, by traders and pirates
Until then at last we met up with Warspite

Repeat chorus

Then the signal was made for the Task Group to anchor
In nebula hidden that night for to lie
Secure all the warp cores, OCPs and crystals
And prep your reactors, for tomorrow we fly!

Repeat chorus

Now let every sailor toss off a full bumper
And let every sailor drink up their full tot
We'll drink and be merry and drown melancholy
With a health to this jovial and true-hearted lot
