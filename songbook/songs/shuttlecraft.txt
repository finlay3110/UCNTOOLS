The smallest ship that's in our fleet, It is the shuttle craft
It's crewed by four with room for more, In an emergency
We undock from our mothership, And make our way alone
And if we're really lucky, They'll not know where we've gone

Chorus:
And shall the shuttle live
Or shall the shuttle die
Cambell's engineering corps
Shall know the reason why

A good hull full of trusty hands, Well train-ed, brave and true
All here to show the captain here, What our cadets can do
No matter when or where we go, Across the inky black
Through peril bleak and sneak attack, We'll bring our shuttle back

Repeat chorus

Well the pilot they do fly the bird, it's almost point and click
The engineer moves slides around, it's enough to make you sick
The generalist is over-worked, bereft of enough hands
The XO will just stand right there, whilst barking out commands

Repeat chorus
