title: The Smallest Ship
category: Bar/Drinking Songs
words: Lt. Lefe Grds
tune: The Song of the Western Men; Louisa T Clare

A good hull full of trusty hands
Well train-ed, brave and true
All here to show the mothership
What our cadets can do
No matter when or where we go
Across the inky black
Through peril bleak and sneak attack
We'll bring our shuttle back

Chorus:
And shall the shuttle live
Or shall the shuttle die
The captain of our mothership
Shall know the reason why

A pirate Captain brave and bold
Our shuttle eye'd with glee
In tractor beam our bird was hold
We could not quite break free
The mothership in hot pursuit
"Adamas is no stay"
We'll "go beyond" in unity
Your enemies to slay

Repeat chorus

And when they came to their foul lair
And came under attack
No quarter will our captain give
Until our shuttle's back
Now shuttle is a tough old bird
But the damage is quite great
Blown OCPs and safety smoke
Signal a dire fate

Repeat chorus
