Should old acquaintance be forgot
And never brought to mind
Should old acquaintance be forgot
And auld lang syne?

For auld lang syne

We crew have warped about these skies
And seen such sites sublime
But we've travelled many a parsec cold
Since auld lang syne

Repeat chorus

We crew have faced foes bold and strange
Across all space and time
But we've been scattered 'cross the void
Since auld lang syne

Repeat chorus

And there's a hand my trusty friend
And give me a hand o' thyne
And we'll take a right good-will draught
For auld lang syne
