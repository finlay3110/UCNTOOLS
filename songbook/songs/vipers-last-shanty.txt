title: Vipers’ Last Shanty
category: Bar/Drinking Songs
words: Lt Cmdr Freddie Moore / Lt Beth
tune: The Last Shanty; Tom Lewis
---
Well Adebayo told me when first I joined the Fleet,
We’re out here ‘cause of Gravium, we need much more of it.
Then last year ‘Vipers’ showed up and now we are at war,
All officers to Warspite Mess, with them we’ll wipe the floor!

Chorus:
Don't break up the warp, thro’ asteroids don’t crash,
And if you see a yellow ship, it might be your last.
Just get your flight suit ready for another run-aboard,
An ensign ain't an ensign, ain't an ensign anymore.

We teleported light years, got a cocktail from the Mess,
Our TO told us use the head, no other time to rest.
A briefing gives our heading, to the docking bay we head,
Through engineer and bridge systems we are so nobly led.

Repeat chorus

Left to our own devices, set course for waypoint one,
Encountering McGillivray, Marines or Blake Paxton.
The war has made us worried, but friends are close at hand,
Former foes have joined our cause, together we do stand.

Repeat chorus

The frontline’s getting scary, so much yellow on the board,
They’ve killed too many of us, we vow to add no more.
We’ll make the deals with who we need, humanity’s at stake,
Flight Control we need some help, back home we need to make!

Repeat chorus

Two nukes to fire a day and that's your bleeding lot.
Then home to the Warspite Mess because we’ve GOT The Tot!
Forget about your civvies for a couple hours or more,
An officer’s an officer just like he was before.

Repeat chorus
