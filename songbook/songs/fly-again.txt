She was boarded during Wargames, she was wracked by Uvie beams
The skipper gave themselves up, to thwart their evil schemes
A sense of duty unsurpassed, they knew they had to go
Their loss had dealt us all a mighty blow

The evacuation shuttles launched, though some marines were lost
Most of the crew was safe then, but still we count the cost
And when we heard the Herald's voice, it caused us to proclaim
That the Falchion and her captain'll fly again

Now Hamilton's beloved, there's no price that we won't spend
After such distinguished service, we won't let this be their end
But with frontline battles raging, from Horizon to Sca Fell
We've got to fight the wider war as well

But we talked of them on CommsNet, every day around the clock
We need to get them back and then to Harden and the docs
And with every jar that hit the bar, we swore we would remain
And make sure Falchion and her captain fly again

Fly again, fly again!
Let their names be ever spoken throughout the UCN
Those who love them best and will be with 'em 'til the end
Will make sure Falchion and her captain fly again

The Falchion's at Kalgoorlie now, her hull all warped and bent
Three shifts a day are working on her, not to mention Dent
But even if we're short on parts, and fixing her is slow
Our plan to rescue Hamilton's a go

The intel's sound, crews inbound, the Tak and Havock prepped
The Herald's ship's been spotted and we're poised to intercept
Tomorrow then, our ships deploy, our mission here is plain
To make sure Falchion and her captain fly again

Fly again, fly again!
