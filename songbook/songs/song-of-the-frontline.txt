Come, cheer up, my crew, 'tis to glory we steer
To add something new to this wonderful year;
To honour we call you, the best of our race
For who are so free as those out here in space

Chorus:
Hulls of steel are our ships,
Of the bold UCN
We always stand ready - steady, crew, Steady!
We'll fight and we'll beat 'em again and again

We ne'er see our foes across the vastness of space
They never see us as they don't dare to face
If they run, why we follow at warp factor 3
For the chase is an action we all love to see

Repeat chorus

They say they'll invade us, our bold enemies,
The pirates, rogue factions and UVPs,
But if they in their hulls plan any attack
We of this task group will push them right back

Repeat chorus

We still make them fear and we still make them flee
We'll meet them in space to beat easily
Then cheer up my crew with this message to send
Our cadets, our captains to our president

Repeat chorus
