At the heart of the ship there's a crucial site
Lever, it's a lever
And the ensigns want to know what it's like
But please do not pull the lever

Chorus:
Do not pull the lever,
Oh do not pull the lever
For the voyage is long if you're at sublight
Which you'll be if you pull the lever

Our TO pointed at this control:
"Lever, it's the lever"
"You should fix the drive if it's going to blow"
"But please do not pull the lever"

Repeat chorus

When J.U.D.I.T.H. alerts please do not take fright
Do not pull the lever
Just switch the right switches, it'll be all right
But please do not pull the lever

Repeat chorus

If the countdown's low and the drive's awry
Maybe pull the lever
'Cause if it implodes then we all might die
So it's time to pull the lever

Lever, pull the lever
Oh lever, pull the lever
And the aft of the ship fills with safety smoke
When you pull the lever
