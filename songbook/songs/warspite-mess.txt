Oh, farewell to you dear Doctor, ten thousand times adieu;
We're off to board the Havock now, as a part of its fine crew;
Once more to part from you, fine ship, it's the mess that I adore.
But still I live in hopes to see the Warspite Mess once more.

Chorus:
The Warspite Mess once more, fine ship, with the crew that I adore,
But still I live in hopes to see old Warspite's mess once more.

Oh! It's now that we are out in space, and docks are far behind,
Comm section will transmit to you of the secrets of my mind,
The secrets of my mind, fine crew, of the ship that I adore,
But still I live in hopes to see the Warspite Mess once more.

Repeat chorus

Oh now this pulsar's blinding, with asteroids coming on,
The radar's dead and impulse too, now our shields all are gone;
The good old Havock is tossed back, the great hull is all tore,
But still I live in hopes to see the Warspite Mess once more.

Repeat chorus

Oh, it's now that time warp's over and we are safe once more;
We'll drink strong drinks and brandies too, to the ships that we adore.
To the ships that we adore, fine crews, we'll make this mess hall roar,
And when our credits are all gone we'll go to space for more.

Repeat chorus
