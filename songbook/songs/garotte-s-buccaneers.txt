title: Garotte's Buccaneers

O the year was twenty one eighty two
How I wish I was on Xanadu now
A letter of marque came from the Gliese
To the scummiest vessel I've ever seen

Chorus:
God damn them all
I was told we'd cruise the belt for lost Jasa gold
We'd fire no beams, shed no tears
Now I'm a broken man with no cocktail here
The last of Garotte's Buccaneers

O Elcid Garotte cried the town
How I wish I was on Xanadu now
For 20 good spacers void trained who
Would make for him the Antelope's crew

Repeat chorus

The Antelope sloop was a sickening sight
How I wish I was on Xanadu now
She'd list to the port and a deck that sags
And the cook in the scuppers with the staggers and jags

Repeat chorus

On Alta's birthday we put to space
How I wish I was on Xanadu now
Too many hours to find our prey
Juggling coolant all the way

Repeat chorus

When the second watch woke we sailed again
How I wish I was on Xanadu now
When a UCN freighter hove in sight
With our cracked torp-tubes we made to fight

Repeat chorus

The UCN weighed down with tin
How I wish I was on Xanadu now
She was broad and fat and slow as heck
And to catch her took the Antelope two parsecs

Repeat chorus

Then at length she stood two sectors away
How I wish I was on Xanadu now
Our cracked torp tubes made awful din
With one fat missile they stove us in

Repeat chorus

The Antelope shook and pitched on her side
How I wish I was on Xanadu now
Garotte was smashed like a bowl of eggs
A lethal beam carried off both me legs

Repeat chorus

Now here I lay in my twenty-third year
How I wish I was on Xanadu now
It's been two months since we sailed away
And I just made Roma yesterday

Repeat chorus
