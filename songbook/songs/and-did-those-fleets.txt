And did those fleets in ancient time
Warp into Gliese's system belt:
And was the gravium they found
The source of renewed hope they felt

And did the mining ships of old
Dig deep into the asteroids
And was Xanadu builded here
As people spread across the void

Attune our beams of burning light
Load our tubes with HVLIs
Set all our shield frequencies right
Bring us in range and prep to fire!

We will not cease from astral fight
Nor from our solemn duty hide
Until humanity safely sleeps
In Sol and Gliese all system-wide
