id: 33
title: The Comms Man and the Grey Hat
category: Bar/Drinking Songs
words: Lt. Cmdr. "Toast" Ray & Cadet Simon Clark
tune: William Taylor; trad
source_book: UCN Songbook
source_page: 36
---
The comms man and his grey hat lover
Full of mirth and loyalty
They were going to the church to be married
She was pressed to the wine dark sea

He dressed in sailor's clothing
Boarded off the Warspite
For to find the life they'd stolen
And the woman he did adore

Chorus:
Dally-dilly-dum, dilly-dum-dum-dum-dum
Dally-dilly-dum, dilly-dum-dum-day
Dally-dilly-dum, dilly-dum-dum-dum-dum
Dally-dilly-dum, dilly-dum-dum-day

Said the captain to this cadet
"What misfortune brought you here?"
"I'm in search of my true lover
Whom they pressed on the other year"

"If she is your true lover
She has proved to you untrue
She is married to another
Left you here alone to rue"

Repeat chorus

He got up the very next morning
Early as the break of day
There he spied his Grey Hat lover
Racing along with her new love's drone

He threw out a brace of missiles
That he had at his command
There he shot his Grey Hat lover
With her love at her right hand

When the captain he found him and the deed that he had done
He made him the cocktail waiter for the drinks of a hundred men

Repeat chorus

The comms man and his Grey Hat lover
Full of mirth and loyalty
She is buried, cold and lonesome
He still sails the wine dark sea

Repeat chorus
