We are the Warspite Task Group and we always do our best
We can fly and fight and explore and we hardly need to rest
And when we’ve done our crewing we stick medals to our vest
And we’ll just keep flying on…

Chorus:
Glory to the UCN
Glory to the UCN
Glory to the UCN
And we’ll always Go Beyond!

We have the Takanami, she’s a big ship strong and true
But you want to go quickly we’ve a sorry tale for you
But why people dislike her, why we haven’t got a clue
So we’ll just keep flying on…

Repeat chorus

We’ve also got the Havock, she a fast ship of the fleet
And the railings on the bridge help keep the Captain in their seat
But while she is not that powerful, she still is really neat
And we’ll just keep flying on…

Repeat chorus

Our friends are Terra Novans they are nice and kind and fair
We don’t know where their planet is, and we don’t really care
Because they make such lovely food they always like to share
So we’ll just keep flying on…

Repeat chorus

Then there’s the Grey Hat Hackers they’re a nasty piece of work
Their drones are real annoying and their Boss talks like a jerk
So we’ll blow up all their ships and we will do it with a smirk
Then we’ll just keep flying on…

Repeat chorus

There’s the scary Cult of Torment, they like throwing things to space
They really are quite crazy and we’ll say it to their face
Because killing people’s not on so we’ll put them in their place
And we’ll just keep flying on…
repeat chorus

They came in from the Barrens, they're the scary UVPs!
All the Vipers want to do is bring all humans to their knees,
But we're not gonna give up, we're as daring as you please,
So we'll just keep fighting on…

Repeat chorus

We hang out in the Mess to get some lovely R&R
But the coinage of an officer don’t really stretch that far
So we play games, laugh and sing and order nice drinks from the bar
Then we’ll just keep flying on…

Repeat chorus
