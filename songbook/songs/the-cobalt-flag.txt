The UCN flag flies in space
A symbol of the human race
In venturing we shall be bold
Through void of space both dark and cold

Chorus:
So let the Cobalt Banner fly
From star to star across the sky
What perils face us we don't know
In the beyond where we must go

From many nations of the Earth
Confederation had its birth
Then Luna, moving on to Mars
And then beyond to reach the stars

Repeat chorus

For friends we find across the belt
Felicitations truly felt
With efforts bold and allies true
We proudly fly our banner blue

Repeat chorus

From nebula storm or astral rocks
We'll brook no temporal paradox
Events must happen now and then
We'll do before what's done again

Repeat chorus

Across the void our ships shall range
Locating dangers dire and strange
Time and again our crews come back
To face what lurks out in the black

Repeat chorus

We came for peace, prepared for war
We face a foe like none before
On this we are all of one mind
We'll leave no officer behind

Repeat chorus

Though war has cost us many a friend
We will not falter, break or bend
Unwavering we all shall be
We fight for all humanity

Repeat chorus
