He was just a rookie ensign, fresh off the transporter pad
He boarded the starship Havock, with adventures to be had
The captain, brave and grizzled, gave a nod and a sly wink,
He'll be back to the belt for sure!

Chorus:
Glory, glory, Havock's a hell of a ship to fly,
Glory, glory, Havock's a hell of a ship to fly,
Glory, glory, Havock's a hell of a ship to fly,
He'll be back to the belt for sure!

The plucky crew they cheered him, as he strapped into his seat,
A new adventure waiting, a brand-new foe to meet.
They set a course and jumped to warp, the stars began to blur,
He'll be back to the belt for sure!

Repeat chorus

As they travelled through the Adamas Belt the pirates laid their evil trap,
The Havock's shields were tested, with nary time to nap.
The battle raged and cannons roared, a fight to hold their ground,
He'll be back to the belt for sure!

Repeat chorus

The warp core starts to shudder, with a blinding flash of red,
"Shields Down!" cried the ensign, filled with growing sense of dread.
They wrestled with the systems, as the sparks began to fly,
He'll be back to the belt for sure!

Repeat chorus

With skill and steady courage, the ensign saved the day,
The Havock limped to safety, escaping from harm's way.
The captain looked upon his crew with a proud and knowing grin,
He'll be back to the belt for sure!

Repeat chorus

Back on board the Warspite, where the dispatches were read
A shiny new promotion, upon the ensign's head.
From rookie to Lieutenant, a hero through and through,
He'll be back to the belt for sure!
