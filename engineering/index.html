    System Effectiveness

    How additional power allocation affects each system's performance. Power levels are not capped at 100% — systems can be overdriven well beyond it, at a coolant cost (see the Thermal & Power Guide).

    Read it in the manual: 3.1 Power, Heat, Coolant & Repairs

    SystemEffect of Power Increase
    ReactorPower generation increases effectiveness, with diminishing returns.
    BeamsRate of fire scales linearly.
    MissilesReload speed scales linearly.
    ManoeuvringRotation speed scales linearly.
    ImpulseForward/backward speed scales linearly.
    WarpLinear for power drain; non-linear in practical use.
    Shield GeneratorRecharge speed scales linearly; defensive return is non-linear.
    ScannerScanner range and alignment time scale linearly; increased power dramatically increases drone range.
    Cargo DocksInter-dock transfer speed and tractor beam movement scale linearly.

    When to Call a Shield Spike

    A shield generator takes about 7 seconds to reach 300%, so a shield spike should start about 8 seconds before impact. From the UCN Academy Manual (Table E-2).

    IncomingCall the Spike When It’s
    HVLI (slug)About 4U out
    Torpedo (tracker)About 1.5–1.6U out
    NukeAbout 1.5–1.6U out
    EMPAbout 1.5–1.6U out, unless you plan to drop shields for it

    Closing on the ordnance, call earlier; moving away, the window is slightly longer. In combat, slightly early beats perfectly late.

    Overheating With No Coolant

    How fast a system heats at each power level with no coolant, and how long it takes to go from 0° to 100°, where damage starts. From the UCN Academy Manual (Table 3-3).

    Power LevelHeat per MinuteOverheats In
    100%Slight coolingNever
    150%~88°~1m 08s
    200%~207°~29s
    230%~295°~20s
    250%~362°~17s
    300%~564°~11s

    These assume the system is already at that power. Power changes ramp over several seconds, so real spikes last slightly longer.

    Spike Hold Times

    How long a spike can be held before resetting, starting cold with power and coolant raised together and the coolant left on through the reset. From the UCN Academy Manual (Table E-1).

    Read it in the manual: Appendix E: Power Spikes

    Spike Power0% Coolant25% Coolant50% Coolant75% Coolant100% Coolant
    150%~67s~7m 40sIndefiniteIndefiniteIndefinite
    200%~26s~44s~1m 47sIndefiniteIndefinite
    250%~12s~18s~27s~44s~1m 41s
    300%~5s~8s~11s~15s~22s

    Power takes about 7 seconds to climb from 100% to 300% and another 7 to come back down, adding about 26° on the way. These times allow for that: reset on time and the system tops out at about 70–95°. Coolant moves about a second slower than power, so apply it just before the spike.

    Field Setups

    Tried-and-tested setups from the UCN Effective Guide to Warp, for when the table above isn’t the whole story.

    Long distance & heat effective

    Warp2
    Warp power136%
    Warp coolant100%
    Top speed2.72U/s
    Warp drain3.1 energy/s
    Total drain3.6 energy/s
    Distance416U (~21 sectors)

    The warp drive never builds heat in this setup: only the energy stored on the ship limits you. You’ll arrive with drained power cells, but you’ll get there.

    Very long distance

    Warp1
    Warp149% power, 70% coolant
    Reactor149% power, 30% coolant
    Manoeuvring100% power
    Scanners100% power
    Every other system0% power
    Top speed1.49U/s
    DistanceUnlimited

    Everything except manoeuvring and scanners is switched off; those two stay online so you can avoid obstacles and scan incoming enemies. To keep more systems online, put more power and coolant into the reactor and take some from warp. The balance point is fine, so err on the side of the reactor.

    Warp 4 quick

    Warp4
    Warp power300%
    Warp coolant100%
    Top speed12U/s (theoretical)
    Max time20 seconds
    Distance110U (~5.5 sectors)

    Warning: this will completely destroy your warp drive. Don’t take it lightly, and drop out of warp before the 20 seconds are up. If you don’t, you may not be able to leave warp 4: the drive keeps overheating and destroys itself as you try to repair it.

    How to Pull a Sick Donut

    1. Increase manoeuvring to max on Engineering.
    2. Go into warp 3.
    3. Turn heavily, 180°.
    4. Combat manoeuvre to the side, and adjust to hold the circle.
    5. Come out of warp.

    Do not tell Dent if manoeuvre performed.
