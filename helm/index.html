Impulse is timed from a standstill, speeding up to full over 5 seconds. Warp is 1U a second per level at 100% power; allow a few seconds more for the drive to spin up (the warp table’s times include it).

Bearings and turns

Put it astern

The shorter way round. Bearings run clockwise, so a clockwise turn is to starboard. “Put it astern” is the heading that turns your back on the target: the reciprocal of its bearing.

Combat manoeuvre

  • Forward boost heats impulse; a sideways strafe heats manoeuvring; a diagonal heats both.
  • A full boost or strafe empties the reserve in about 3 seconds, at about 20° of heat a second (50–60° in all).
  • A diagonal empties it in about 1.5 seconds, putting 25–30° of heat into each.
  • It doesn’t work at warp.
