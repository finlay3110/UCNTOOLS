How to play

  1. Pick a security level. Each one hides a set number of traps in its grid of nodes.
  2. Probe a node by tapping it. Your first probe is always safe, and opens up part of the grid.
  3. Read the numbers. A number tells you how many of the 8 nodes touching it (diagonals too) hide a trap. A blank node has none, so its neighbours open by themselves.
  4. Probe every safe node to gain access. Probe a trap and the trace catches you. As in Bridge Command there are no flags, so keep track of the traps in your head.
  5. Beat your records. The clock starts on your first probe. Your best time and best score for each level are kept below the grid.

Tips & tricks

  • Work along the edge of the open area. Numbers there touch the fewest hidden nodes, so they give the surest answers.
  • Full house: if a number has exactly that many hidden nodes around it, every one of them is a trap. Leave them all alone.
  • Already satisfied: once you know where all of a number's traps are, every other hidden node around it is safe. Probe them.
  • Compare neighbours: when one number's hidden nodes are all shared with the number next to it, any extra nodes the second one touches hold the difference. Equal numbers mean the extras are safe.
  • 1-2-1 on a straight edge: the traps sit opposite the two 1s, and the node opposite the 2 is safe.
  • 1-2-2-1 on a straight edge: the traps sit opposite the two 2s, and the nodes opposite the 1s are safe.
  • Finish one area before the next. With no flags to mark them, the traps you've worked out are easy to lose track of. Settle a corner completely, then move on.
  • Count the traps near the end. Each level has a fixed number (shown in the level list). If the traps you know about account for all of them, every other hidden node is safe.
  • When you must guess (it happens on Hardened and Fortified, where a quarter of the nodes are traps), guess early rather than after lots of work, and pick a node that would tell you the most if it's safe.
  • Train up through the levels. On Basic and Secure, practise spotting the patterns until you see them at a glance. That's where fast times come from.
