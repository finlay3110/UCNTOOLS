The drills

  • Radar recognition: ship icons and the manual's advice for each, contact colours, ordnance marks and the radar's signal channels.
  • Ordnance choice: which weapon suits the moment, and the ranges, timings and blasts behind the choice.
  • Callouts: the standard call for the moment, station by station. Short, addressed and specific beats vague every time.
  • Damage Control: where each OCP, crystal and warp switch is on the Takanami and the Havock (the deck plan shows it circled once you answer), then OCPs, crystals, manual repairs and warp core emergencies.

Each round is ten questions in a random order. Tap the answer you'd give: the right one turns green, with the manual's reason. The clock runs from the first question to the last answer, so a quick, accurate round beats a slow perfect one only on time.

Questions from the unofficial UCN Academy Manual; ship icons from the Navigation & Radar tool. Fan-made training game for the UCN tools. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by Bridge Command / The London Space Elevator Limited.
