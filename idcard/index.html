Get it printed on plastic

The Plastic Card Factory prints real PVC ID cards. Download both print files, then on their site choose Create ID Cards and a landscape blank layout. Upload the front file as the Background Image on page 1, and the back file on page 2.

Made to their artwork guide: a CR80 card with 3mm bleed all round (92×60mm), CMYK colours, and vector text and codes that print sharp at any DPI. Before printing logos you don't own, they ask for proof of permission, and the UCN logo belongs to Bridge Command.
